4. **Light Theme Default**: Guarantee Light mode surfaces are bright and crisp, with proper borders and contrast, keeping the canvas workspace as a neutral dark production frame.
5. **Stream Check & Immutability**: Harden preflight checklist and serialize immutable scene snapshots upon stream creation.
